# CancerOmicsLake Manuscript Package

For the current PhD interview presentation, use
[`interview_brief_v2/brief.pdf`](interview_brief_v2/brief.pdf) and its
[four-page LaTeX source](interview_brief_v2/brief.tex), updated 19 October 2026.
Revision 2 adds workflow/design diagrams, uncertainty intervals and explicit
study denominators; the first revision remains under `interview_brief/`.
[The dated research update](research_update_2026-10-19.md) records the later
feasibility results and the current scope of the contribution.

The broader aggregate evidence review and specimen-level inputs are maintained
separately from this outreach artifact. The brief ledger records source hashes;
the self-contained LaTeX archive can be compiled without those inputs.

This directory is generated by `make build-manuscript-package`.

- `manuscript.md`: journal-neutral methods/data-engineering draft
- `tables/`: manuscript-facing aggregate CSV tables
- `figures/`: editable SVG figures
- `supplement/`: full multi-K results and reproducibility checklist
- `evidence_ledger.json`: claim-to-source mapping with SHA-256 hashes
- `manuscript_metadata.yml`: exact author/disclosure metadata used to render the draft

Complete and confirm `configs/manuscript_metadata.yml`, obtain biological review, and re-run the
generator after any evidence-producing pipeline change.
